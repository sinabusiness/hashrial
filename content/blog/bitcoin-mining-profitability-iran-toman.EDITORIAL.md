# Fact-check before publishing: bitcoin-mining-profitability-iran-toman

Status is `draft` in both language files. Change to `published`
only after working through the list below.

Fact-check before publishing. (1) MARKET INPUTS — BTC at $110,000, free-market USD at 194,000 toman, and network hashrate at ~1,000 EH/s are the assumptions the whole article is built on. They are labelled as assumptions in a table, but update them to the real figures on publication day and re-run the arithmetic. All downstream numbers derive from hashprice = $0.050/TH/day (9,700 toman gross, 9,506 net of 2%); if you change an input, every table changes. A script that regenerates the tables from the four inputs would be worth having if this becomes a recurring piece. (2) TRANSACTION FEES — I assumed fees add ~1.5% to block subsidy revenue. Verify against recent mempool data; it swings. (3) ELECTRICITY TARIFF — the article deliberately does NOT state a current tariff, and this is the right posture. The structural claims (quarterly reset pegged to export price and an FX conversion rate; prohibited in critical-peak, ×2 restricted, ×0.5 normal; ~20% discount at 400/230 kV, ~12% at 132/66/63 kV) come from the competitor research, which traced them to a 1400/2021-era announcement via alominer.com and peivast.com. I could not verify them against a primary Tavanir or Ministry of Energy source. Someone must check the current regulation before publishing — if the multiplier structure has changed, the "restricted hours flip the S19 negative" argument changes with it. The 16,574 rials/kWh figure is presented explicitly as an outdated number being debunked, not as current, which is the safest framing, but confirm the figure was really ever the announced base rate. (4) LEGAL — the residential/agricultural-connection warning is deliberately vague and points the reader to a lawyer. Do not let anyone "improve" it into a specific claim about penalties. (5) HARDWARE SPECS — S19j Pro 104 TH/3,068 W, S19 XP 141 TH/3,010 W, S21 200 TH/3,500 W, S21 Pro 234 TH/3,510 W are manufacturer nameplate figures; worth a spot-check against Bitmain's current sheets since sub-variants exist. (6) HYPOTHETICAL PRICE — the 850 million toman S21 cost in the payback section is explicitly flagged as a placeholder. If you have real Iranian street pricing, substitute it; if not, leave the flag in place. (7) DO NOT ADD a "best pools" recommendation section to this article. Separately: khanesarmaye.com's top-ranking Farsi pool listicle currently recommends Poolin, which filed Chapter 11 on 22 July 2026. That is a strong basis for a different article, but verify the bankruptcy filing details independently before publishing anything that names it. (8) The Braiins relationship is not mentioned in this article by design — the keyword research found zero Persian search demand for the brand in any transliteration.
